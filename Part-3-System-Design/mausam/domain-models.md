# 3. Domain Models

Domain models capture business concepts, attributes, and associations without committing to implementation classes. The following diagrams follow the concept-centered approach illustrated by Marsic Figures 2-25 and 2-26.

## 3.1 Catalog and Inventory Domain Model

![Figure 3.1 - Catalog and Inventory Domain Model](catalog-inventory-domain.png)

**Figure 3.1.** Domain concepts and business associations for Catalog and Inventory.

Interpretation: the diagram identifies persistent business information and its relationships. Multiplicities are initial analysis assumptions; transaction and stock consistency rules remain authoritative. A conceptual entity should not be mistaken for a UI component or an already implemented database table.
