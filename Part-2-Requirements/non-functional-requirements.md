# Small Mart Automation - Non-Functional Requirements

**Course:** COSC-3506-F01X
**Prepared by:** Uttam Magar

## Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | PW | Size | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR01 | NF | 5 | M | The system shall restrict privileged actions to authorized roles and maintain an audit record. |
| NFR02 | NF | 5 | M | The POS shall provide a fast, low-click item lookup and checkout interface; performance target to be validated in fieldwork. |
| NFR03 | NF | 4 | L | Core POS shall support temporary offline transactions and reconcile safely on reconnection. |
| NFR04 | NF | 5 | M | Sales and stock updates shall remain consistent; failed transactions shall not partially deduct inventory. |

PW = Priority Weight (5 is highest).
Size: S = Small, M = Medium, L = Large.
