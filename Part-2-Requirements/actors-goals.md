# Small Mart Automation - Actors and Goals

**Course:** COSC-3506-F01X
**Prepared by:** Uttam Magar

## Actor-Goals Table

| Actor | Goal | Associated Use Cases |
|---|---|---|
| Manager / Owner | Maintain products, approve orders, manage staff and review operations | UC01, UC05-UC09 |
| Cashier | Complete checkout and returns; support loyalty at sale | UC03, UC04, UC10, UC11 |
| Inventory Clerk | Receive and monitor stock | UC02, UC05 |
| Customer | Purchase items and optionally earn/redeem points | UC10 |
| System / sync service (supporting) | Reconcile offline queued transactions | UC11 |
