# Small Mart Automation — Functional Requirements

Course: COSC-3506-F01X
Part 2: Requirements Documentation
Prepared by: Uttam Magar

## Priority and Size
Priority weight (PW): 1–5, where 5 is highest.
Size: S = Small, M = Medium, L = Large.

## Functional Requirements

| ID | Type | PW | Size | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REQ01 | F | 5 | M | The system shall authenticate staff and enforce role-based access. |
| REQ02 | F | 5 | M | The manager shall create and update product records, prices and reorder thresholds. |
| REQ03 | F | 5 | L | The inventory clerk shall receive shipments and record quantities, batches and expiry dates. |
| REQ04 | F | 5 | M | The system shall maintain current stock quantities following inventory changes. |
| REQ05 | F | 5 | L | The cashier shall process checkout with barcode or SKU lookup and tax/discount calculation. |
| REQ06 | F | 5 | M | The system shall deduct sold quantities after a completed sale and record the transaction. |
| REQ07 | F | 4 | M | The cashier shall process authorized returns and restore eligible stock. |
| REQ08 | F | 5 | S | The system shall generate a receipt for each completed sale. |
| REQ09 | F | 4 | M | The system shall flag products below reorder thresholds or nearing expiry. |
| REQ10 | F | 4 | M | The manager shall review and approve supplier purchase-order recommendations. |
| REQ11 | F | 3 | M | The manager shall manage employee profiles, schedules and clock events. |
| REQ12 | F | 4 | M | The manager shall view sales and inventory summary reports. |
| REQ13 | F | 3 | M | The system shall log voids, overrides and inventory discrepancies for review. |
| REQ14 | F | 2 | M | Customers may earn and redeem loyalty points when the extension is enabled. |

Source: Small Mart Automation — Part 2 Requirements Documentation, Section 2.
